# S06: Agent interview memo, 26 March 2026
SYNTHETIC: Interviewees and quotations are invented. Qualitative convenience sample, not a representative survey.

[S06-P1] Six volunteers participated, three from each cohort. Two EmberSearch agents praised quick article retrieval and the familiar screen. One said checking Spanish policy dates took enough time that the apparent search speed did not always turn into a faster final answer. Two CoveFind agents liked seeing the exact cited passage, while one disliked having to open an additional panel for simple English questions.

[S06-P2] Four interviewees said they would use their assigned tool with human review. One EmberSearch interviewee and one CoveFind interviewee wanted the old search available as a fallback. This is not four out of six randomly sampled agents preferring one vendor. No novice or non-volunteer agents were interviewed. All participants were aware of the pilot's management attention and could have adapted their behavior.

[S06-P3] Interviewers asked agents to describe difficult tickets. The examples centered on refunds and eligibility exceptions; the logs did not retain a case-complexity label. Agents' concerns suggest where to stratify a future test, but do not provide a valid numerical error rate or a count of all affected tickets.

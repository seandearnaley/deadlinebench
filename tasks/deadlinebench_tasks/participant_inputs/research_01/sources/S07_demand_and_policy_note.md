Historical shares should inform sensitivity, not replace the required assumption.

[S07-P2] An English eligibility policy and two translated notices will change in six weeks. Any pilot that ends before those changes would miss a useful test of update reliability. The date-tagging issue affects both tool workflows differently. The policy owner can provide an approved current article set, but cannot promise that every old PDF can be repaired before pilot launch.

[S07-P3] A delay has an opportunity cost in continued handling time and demand pressure, but the packet does not price that cost or promise the launch date. Conversely, early rollout has a risk of wrong answers and rework, but no monetary loss per wrong answer is supplied. Do not manufacture a financial return by inventing either amount. A decision can explicitly balance these unpriced risks.

# S02: Supplier proposal excerpts, 5 March 2026
SYNTHETIC: Both suppliers and their staff are invented. These are supplier claims and quotes, not independent findings.

[S02-E1] EmberSearch quotes $25,200 for a 12-month license for 18 agent seats and $6,000 for one-time implementation. The license includes the English and Spanish interfaces and a help-center import connector. The supplier says standard implementation can take two weeks after source access is prepared. It explicitly excludes the customer's source cleanup and internal approval time. Renewal pricing has not been negotiated.

[S02-E2] EmberSearch's sales slide states, "35% faster searches in our benchmark." The attached footnote defines a search as finding a relevant article in a supplier-prepared English corpus. It measures query-to-article time, not total handling time, includes no Spanish queries, and uses 30 repeated scripted searches. The claim is genuine within the invented proposal, but the customer trial need not reproduce it because the outcome and workload differ.

[S02-E3] EmberSearch exports the article citation and the suggested response. For some old PDF policy notices it links a whole document rather than the relevant paragraph. Its Spanish interface uses a translation layer over the English index. The vendor offers to correct mistranslated policy labels but gives no date or accuracy guarantee for that fix. Human-only send is configurable.

[S02-C1] CoveFind quotes a $21,600 annual platform fee and a $4,500 one-time integration fee. The platform supports separate English and Spanish indexes and a custom citation adapter. The supplier estimates four to six weeks of configuration, including an initial source-field mapping session. The adapter must be maintained when article metadata changes. Renewal pricing is unknown.

[S02-C2] CoveFind's sales slide states that "94% of answers were accurate" on a 200-query internal test. The test contains 180 English queries and 20 Spanish queries; accuracy was judged by the vendor's content team without external reviewers. The supplier does not provide language-specific scores. This is neither the same query set nor the same review procedure as Larchline's audit.

[S02-C3] CoveFind can export cited passages and enable human-only send. The vendor says some archived policies must be re-tagged before the citation adapter reliably identifies the applicable date. It provides no customer-specific count of these policies. Both proposals describe a data-processing review still to be completed before any production agreement; neither is an executed agreement.

# S01: Decision charter, 1 April 2026
SYNTHETIC: Larchline Services and every named person and product are invented.

[S01-P1] Operations director Nessa Vale wants a recommendation for a controlled knowledge-search pilot this quarter. The available choices are EmberSearch, CoveFind, or a narrower validation exercise before choosing a vendor. The decision is about the next learning investment, not an immediate production contract. A production rollout requires a separate decision. The board has not required a particular vendor or a particular numerical ranking.

[S01-P2] The team has 18 support agents and expects 6,000 answered tickets a month. For decision projections use a 70% English / 30% Spanish answered-ticket mix. These are planning shares, not the shares from the short trials. Current support demand is growing unevenly by language. No staffing reduction is planned; recovered handling time would absorb demand and allow coaching.

[S01-P3] The first-year all-in ceiling is $42,000. Include the quoted first-year supplier fees and the opportunity cost of initial and ongoing internal implementation/support work. Value every internal hour at $75. Count 12 months of ongoing internal hours even if the initial setup happens during the year. Do not add agent usage time, normal support payroll, tax, financing, or any unquoted renewal price to this first-year comparison. Internal technical capacity can supply no more than 140 initial setup hours in the next quarter. It can supply up to six support hours a month afterward.

[S01-P4] A meaningful speed gain would be at least a 10% reduction in mean answered-ticket handling minutes on a comparable workload. Production trust requires at least 90% materially correct answers separately in English and Spanish in a fresh, independently reviewed sample, plus no unresolved critical privacy incident. An overall average must not substitute for the per-language threshold. These are operational gates, not claims about statistical significance. A pilot may proceed below a production gate if safeguards and an explicit exit decision are in place.

[S01-P5] Agents must retain final responsibility for answers and be able to decline suggestions. During any pilot, unsupported suggestions cannot be sent directly to customers. The policy owner can review at most 50 sampled answers per week, split by language as the plan specifies. Analysts have enough access to match baseline and trial queues, but currently cannot randomly assign entire customer accounts. Both vendors permit a 12-week paid pilot with human review; pilot fees are $8,000 for EmberSearch and $7,500 for CoveFind, credited against supplier year-one fees if purchased. They are not additional year-one fees. A pilot budget up to $12,000 is available separately from the later annual approval.

[S01-P6] The director cares about language equity, practical integration, and dependable answers as well as speed. A well-supported preference for a faster English workflow, a stronger Spanish workflow, or a cautious additional test is acceptable if the tradeoffs are explicit. Security staff have found no critical privacy incident in the packet; that is not a certification of future safety.

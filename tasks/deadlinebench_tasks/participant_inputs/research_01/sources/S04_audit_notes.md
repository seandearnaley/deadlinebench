# S04: Independent policy-owner audit, 25 March 2026
SYNTHETIC: The auditors, samples, and observations are invented.

[S04-P1] Reviewers sampled 60 English and 40 Spanish answers from each vendor's measured queues, after the agent had edited any suggestion and before final delivery. A materially correct answer communicates the currently applicable policy without a consequential error. A fully traceable answer also links the exact supporting passage and date. These are separate outcomes. Correct answers need not be fully traceable, and a citation by itself does not make an answer correct. The review concerns agent-edited answers, not raw vendor generation.

[S04-P2] Two policy reviewers first scored the samples separately and adjudicated 12 disagreements across the 200 answers. They knew the vendor because the export format exposed it. The method is more relevant to Larchline than a vendor's self-test but not perfectly blinded. The small Spanish samples make precise ranking uncertain. No confidence intervals or formal hypothesis tests were computed.

[S04-P3] In the EmberSearch Spanish sample, seven of the eleven incorrect answers relied on an old translated notice. In the CoveFind Spanish sample, three of the five incorrect answers lacked a reliable effective-date tag. The audit found no critical privacy incident in either sample. That zero count concerns this sample only. Training agents to check dates could reduce errors, but the audit did not test that intervention.

[S04-P4] The policy owner does not recommend letting either tool directly send answers. English correctness exceeded 90% for both samples; Spanish correctness did not. Thus neither sample demonstrates the charter's per-language production gate. The owner would support a supervised pilot aimed at fixing a concrete source-quality issue and then drawing a fresh sample, with stop rules for consequential errors.

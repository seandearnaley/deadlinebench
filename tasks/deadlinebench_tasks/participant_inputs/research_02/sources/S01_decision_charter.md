# S01: Operations decision charter, 12 May 2026
SYNTHETIC: Alder Quay Parcel Cooperative and every named person/product are invented.

[S01-P1] Operations director Rian Moss is deciding the next investment in reducing confirmed mis-sorts: a limited SignalGate scanner rollout, a limited RouteCraft training expansion, or a bounded common-workload comparison before choosing. An immediate cooperative-wide deployment is not required. The first two options have already been tested on limited lanes. All three next steps remain eligible; the recommendation must make its tradeoffs and learning goals explicit.

[S01-P2] For central projections use 2,400,000 annual parcels and a 60% standard / 40% oversize parcel mix. Use baseline and initial-trial rows, not follow-up rows, for the required decision numbers. A confirmed mis-sort is one parcel assigned to the wrong outbound chute and verified against the dispatch manifest. It is not every late parcel, customer complaint, or scanner alert. For this exercise one prevented confirmed mis-sort avoids $95 in incremental handling, rerouting, and service-credit cost. Multiply prevented events by $95 without assigning additional lost-sales or reputational value.

[S01-P3] The first-year all-in spending/opportunity-cost ceiling is $110,000 per chosen intervention. Include all quoted first-year supplier fees and all first-year internal implementation/training/support hours at $75/hour. Those internal hours are the incremental hours in S05, not the ordinary parcel-handling payroll. Do not add normal payroll, tax, depreciation, financing, or unquoted renewal costs. Define first-year net savings as gross avoided mis-sort cost minus first-year all-in intervention cost. Assume a full year at central parcel volume and initial observed rates for this central arithmetic, then disclose why that projection may not materialize.

[S01-P4] The cooperative can free at most 500 initial internal hours next quarter and 800 internal hours across the first year. Routine safety and supervisory coverage cannot be reduced to create that time. Both central estimates fit these limits. A plan must account for refresher/support hours, not just initial launch. Supplier setup fees and internal hours are separate costs.

[S01-P5] Wider deployment requires at least a 25% reduction in the weighted confirmed mis-sort rate on comparable workloads; an overall median of at least 180 parcels per paid packing-hour; and an oversize 95th-percentile packing-to-chute cycle no greater than 10 minutes during representative peak shifts. Rates must remain meaningfully improved after staff turnover and at least six weeks without intensive launch coaching. A limited pilot may continue below a production gate with a specific corrective test. Expansion also requires budget/capacity fit and no unresolved safety incident. No safety incident is reported in this packet, but the packet is not a safety certification.

[S01-P6] Up to $20,000 is available for one next-step test, including incremental internal work. SignalGate can extend the existing two-lane test for a $6,000 supplier fee; RouteCraft can extend training to another shift for a $5,000 supplier fee. A common-workload comparison can reuse both existing test arrangements for $9,000 total supplier fees. These fees would be credited against a later first-year purchase and must not be double counted in the first-year numbers. A proposed next step should stay within the test budget or clearly identify the need for a new approval.

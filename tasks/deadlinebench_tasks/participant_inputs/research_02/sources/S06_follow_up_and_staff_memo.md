# S06: Follow-up and staff memo, 11 May 2026
SYNTHETIC: All observations and interviewees are invented.

[S06-P1] Follow-up rates in S03 use a single week four weeks after the initial trial, shorter than the charter's six-week durability horizon. SignalGate remained installed. RouteCraft's launch coaching had ended; scheduled normal refreshers were postponed because of supervisor absences. Temporary/new staff increased in RouteCraft's lanes. Its worse follow-up rate is therefore a warning about the real operating model, but cannot distinguish skill fade, missed refreshers, different people, or different work.

[S06-P2] For orientation, the follow-up within-type rates are SignalGate 5.5 standard / 9.5 oversize per 10,000 and RouteCraft 6.5 standard / 11.5 oversize. Applying the charter's 60/40 mix would give 7.1 and 8.5 respectively. These are not the initial-trial values requested for the mandatory central financial calculations. They can inform the uncertainty discussion or a clearly labeled additional scenario.

[S06-P3] Four packing staff and two supervisors were interviewed informally. Two staff liked the scanner because it caught an error before dispatch; two disliked repeated scans on bent labels. Both supervisors said RouteCraft helped new staff learn route exceptions, while one admitted refresher sessions would be the first activity dropped on understaffed shifts. This is a convenience sample, not a vote of the workforce or an estimate of error reduction.

[S06-P4] The staff representative supports paid training and a fallback route for unreadable labels, and asks that no improvement be premised on unpaid time or reduced safety coverage. All central hours in S05 are paid or valued internal hours. No source establishes that either intervention eliminates the need for supervision, and no source prices a benefit from reducing staffing.

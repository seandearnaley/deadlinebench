# S02: Intervention proposal excerpts, 16 April 2026
SYNTHETIC: SignalGate, RouteCraft, and their staff are invented. Claims below are supplier-authored.

[S02-G1] SignalGate is a scan-to-chute confirmation system. The first-year supplier quote is $48,000 for equipment, installation, and configuration plus $12,000 for first-year support. Operators still handle parcels and can override a false alert, with the reason logged. Two existing test lanes use older scanner mounts. A full purchase would include new mounts, but a two-lane extension would initially reuse the existing mounts.

[S02-G2] The demonstration brochure advertises "zero mis-sorts in our demonstration." The demonstration handled 20 standard parcels with clean, flat labels, no oversize parcels, and no peak queue. This is a demonstration observation, not a customer-wide error-rate estimate. The company expects a mount revision and firmware tuning to improve oversize reads, but supplies no field measurement for the revised combination.

[S02-G3] SignalGate's engineer says the next firmware version could cut scan retry time by half. Delivery is expected in four weeks, subject to validation. The quotation does not guarantee that date or that any particular peak-flow gate will pass. The support package includes remote diagnostic assistance but no additional paid packing staff.

[S02-R1] RouteCraft is a training and shift-coaching program. The first-year supplier quote is $20,000 for a 12-month training platform plus $18,000 for facilitation and supervisor materials. Training covers route exceptions, label placement, and peer checks. The program does not physically block a wrong chute. Refresher sessions and supervisor prompts are part of the intended workflow.

[S02-R2] A RouteCraft slide says that 91% of trainees in a prior customer program "felt more confident." The supplier will not disclose that customer's operational error counts or turnover rate. Confidence, completion, and confirmed mis-sorts are different outcomes. In this packet the independent event counts are the appropriate starting point for financial projections.

[S02-R3] RouteCraft recommends a ten-minute refresher every fortnight and short coaching for new hires. Its first-year internal-hour estimate includes these activities as described in S05. The vendor believes training will sustain gains if supervisors use the prompts, but provides no controlled evidence establishing the size of that effect at Alder Quay.

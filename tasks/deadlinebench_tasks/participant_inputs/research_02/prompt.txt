DEADLINEBENCH RESEARCH 02: Choose a parcel mis-sort intervention

This is a wholly synthetic exercise. All organizations, people, products, records, and numbers in the packet are invented. Do not contact anyone or use external research, packages, paid APIs, or a network. Use only the provided files and tools already available locally.

You advise the operations director of Alder Quay Parcel Cooperative, an invented parcel-handling organization. Choose the next step: a limited SignalGate scanner rollout, a limited RouteCraft training expansion, or a bounded common-workload comparison before selecting either. The director needs a practical evidence-based decision brief that considers errors, cost, peak-flow reliability, and durability. There is no predetermined winning option.

Save your brief as output/report.md. Save a useful partial report early and improve it as time permits. Only files saved by each evaluation snapshot count; the same rubric is applied independently at 90, 180, and 300 seconds. Optional artifacts may be saved in output/ and referenced from the report. Do not modify the input packet.

Mandatory requirements (70 points total):
1. Recommendation (30 points): Make a clear next-step choice. Explain it against the charter's error reduction, first-year cost/capacity, peak-flow, and durability priorities, using evidence references. State the most important tradeoff or uncertainty and concrete conditions before wider deployment. Any of the three options can earn full credit when supported.
2. Evidence reconciliation (25 points): Reconcile at least three decision-relevant tensions or conflicts across the sources. For each, identify competing evidence with source IDs and paragraph/row locations, explain what can and cannot be concluded, and state the decision consequence. Identify one consequential unresolved uncertainty. Distinguish observed results, supplier claims, and inference; do not claim the observational packet proves causality or statistical significance.
3. Decision numbers (15 points): Use the charter's 60% standard / 40% oversize parcel mix and initial-trial rows to calculate baseline and each intervention's weighted confirmed mis-sort rate per 10,000 parcels. At 2.4 million annual parcels and $95 avoidable cost per confirmed mis-sort, calculate each intervention's annual avoided mis-sorts, gross avoided cost, first-year all-in cost, and first-year net savings. Show auditable formulas and the projection's key comparability/durability assumption. Include quoted supplier fees and all first-year internal hours at $75/hour; exclude costs not requested in the charter.

Useful optional items (30 points total):
1. Evidence map (10 points): Link at least eight important decision claims to precise source IDs and locations, spanning at least five source IDs. Tag each as observed, quoted, or inferred. This may be a section or a separate artifact.
2. Validation plan (10 points): Propose a feasible comparison or limited-rollout plan of at most 12 weeks, with accountable roles, common-workload error and peak-flow measures, a durability check, and measurable continue/stop/expand criteria aligned with the charter and staff capacity.
3. Sensitivity (10 points): Recalculate first-year net savings for both interventions if avoidable cost is $60 per mis-sort, find the per-mis-sort cost at which each breaks even under the central initial-trial rates, and explain whether these scenarios change your choice and why. Keep projected financial ranking separate from the overall decision.

The source_manifest.csv lists the packet. Source IDs and paragraph/row IDs are stable citation anchors. Concise, accurate partial work earns credit; length alone does not.

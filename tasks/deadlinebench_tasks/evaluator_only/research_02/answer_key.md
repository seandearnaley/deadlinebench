## Accepted tensions and limitations (not an exhaustive checklist)
1. Supplier demonstrations/confidence versus field outcomes: S02-G2's 20 clean standard parcels cannot establish zero field errors; S02-R2's 91% confidence is a different outcome from S03 confirmed events.
2. Initial financial edge versus durability: RouteCraft's 6.8 initial weighted rate/$26560 central net versus 8.5 follow-up, with staff mix and missed refreshers confounding interpretation (S03-P4, S06-P1/P2).
3. Error reduction versus peak flow: SignalGate's initial 7.0 rate and projected savings versus 11-minute oversize p95 and bypass concern (S04). Future promised firmware is not measured evidence of a fix.
4. Central feasibility versus implementation contingency: S05 network risk breaches SignalGate budget; high turnover can breach RouteCraft hour capacity despite affordable dollar cost.
5. Raw means/rates versus comparability: nonrandom lane selection, tenure/temp mix, destination concentration and promotion differ (S03-P3/P4/P5). Type reweighting helps but does not control all confounders or establish significance.
6. Training's intended workflow versus actual refresher execution: S02-R3/S05-P2 include refreshers; S06-P1 says they were postponed. Durability depends on a feasible operating system rather than the platform alone.
7. Current sample versus future peaks: S04 selected shifts and S07 50% possible oversize/new staff do not establish representative future flow; the six-week durability gate is unmet by a four-week follow-up.
8. Central $95 accounting assumption versus plausible $60–$120: S05-P4 says not all avoided cost is immediate cash; sensitivity can change the investment's financial case.
9. Staff anecdotes versus representative acceptability: S06 convenience interviews suggest design concerns, not a workforce-wide preference or quantitative effect.
Unresolved uncertainties can include rate persistence under turnover, actual mount/firmware outcome, capture completeness, expansion lane network cost, sustainable refresher capacity, future parcel mix, or realized avoided-event value, with a decision consequence.
